# Team roles

Official Canvas EM Group Projects roster: Arjun Tripathi, Brayden Cunliffe, Carlos Ibarra Ortiz, Chris Swanson Booth, George Tate, Jason Walshe Dickie, Luke Linebaugh, Vaughn Hafner.

These are proposed work areas. Assign owners together; no assignments have been inferred from who is editing the Word document.

| Work area | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Coordination and integration | Unassigned | Internal deadlines, combined deck, submission |
| Train background research | Unassigned | Selected system, verified specifications and citations |
| System explanation | Unassigned | Labeled diagram and intuitive explanation |
| Magnetic field model | Unassigned | Ampère's law, magnetic circuit, assumptions |
| Lift-force analysis | Unassigned | Force derivation, worked example, weight comparison |
| MATLAB/Simulink | Unassigned | Original model, plots with units, comparison to calculation |
| Design implications and ambiguity | Unassigned | Gap behavior, limitations, alternatives and decision rationale |
| Presentation editing and delivery | Unassigned | Readable slides, references, speaker plan, rehearsal |

Field-model, lift-analysis, and simulation owners should agree on geometry, symbols, units, and parameters before working separately. Every technical output needs another teammate to check it. Three or four speakers may be sufficient; everyone contributes even if they do not speak.
